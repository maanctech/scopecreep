# Private-Beta Sales Guide

## Positioning

ScopeLedger helps service businesses recover unbilled revenue by detecting out-of-scope client requests before they become free work. It is an internal evidence and revenue-control system, not a chatbot, collections service, or autonomous billing agent.

## Recommended Entry Offer

Start with a free lookback audit of one representative project. Ask for the governing SOW, a bounded communication export, the hourly or blended rate, project value, and the delivery lead's suspected scope issues. Return a professional-reviewed report; do not present AI estimates as agreed charges.

## Commercial Options

- Free lookback audit for one representative project.
- $1,500 setup plus $750 per month for a monitoring pilot.
- 10-20% of validated recovered revenue only under written attribution and payment terms.
- Custom enterprise scope for multiple delivery units, support, deployment, and connector validation.

ScopeLedger is hosted. The customer supplies no infrastructure: no database, no server, no AI provider key. They bring the governing SOW, the client communications for a project, the rate they bill at, and a professional to approve findings. Analysis costs are ScopeLedger's, absorbed into the monthly fee, so per-analysis provider charges are a margin question rather than a customer line item.

## Demonstration Rules

Use the fictional Northstar Digital Studio / ApertureOps project and label it fictional. Show the $13,475 potential total, then show how the professional approves or rejects evidence and records invoiced or paid status. Never imply the demo revenue was actually recovered.

## Claims Discipline

- Say `verified locally`, `credential-dependent`, `mocked contract`, or `external approval required` exactly as defined in [Status Matrix](status-matrix.md).
- Do not claim live Slack, Google, Microsoft, IMAP, or real-model verification without dated evidence from the current deployment.
- Do not promise legal compliance, security certification, guaranteed recovery, model accuracy, or autonomous billing.
- Describe hosting accurately: ScopeLedger runs the infrastructure and the database, tenant separation is enforced by PostgreSQL row-level security, and analysis text is sent to a third-party AI provider. Do not claim per-firm data export until it exists.

## Private-Beta Qualification

Prefer firms with high project values, written SOWs, clear rates, an internal delivery owner, and willingness to run a controlled manual workflow. Do not onboard a firm that requires a client portal, automated invoicing, unreviewed AI decisions, or unsupported provider integrations as a condition of success.
